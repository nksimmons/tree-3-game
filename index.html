THE NEW TREE

A tree is dots + connections.

The top dot is the root. Every other dot has one parent above it. Branches go downward, never loop, and never join back together.

Find the whole old pattern.

Match every old dot to a different dot of the same color. Follow branches downward. You can skip extra dots and branches, but separate branches must split at the matching dot.

Why TREE(3)?

The 3 means three colors. TREE(3) is the longest possible legal sequence: a finite number so huge we’ll never finish counting it. Our challenge is to make our own sequence last!

The precise rule, for curious grown-ups

We use unordered, rooted trees with exact color matching and infimum-preserving homeomorphic embedding. The earlier root may map to any dot in the later tree. Each edge maps to a downward path; paths from different children must enter different immediate child branches of the matched parent. Equivalently, the map is injective and preserves colors and least common ancestors. Left-to-right order does not matter. This is not ordinary, exact subtree matching.

Tree number i has at least one and at most i dots. No earlier tree may embed in any later tree. Finishing your own sequence doesn’t mean you’ve found the maximum TREE(3).

Based on Harvey Friedman’s formulation of labeled finite trees.
